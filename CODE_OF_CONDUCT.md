# Code of Conduct

CommitBrief is operated by a small team and a closed circle of invited collaborators. We work professionally, respect each other, and expect the same from anyone who interacts with the organization across our repositories, channels, and communications.

## Expectations

- Treat everyone with respect on every channel — repositories, code reviews, issues, email, and social platforms.
- Disagree on technical substance, not on people. Direct feedback at the work, not the author.
- Give constructive feedback and receive it without taking it personally. Assume good intent.
- Respect confidentiality. CommitBrief handles editorial drafts, source data, scoring weights, and pre-release intelligence. Internal material stays internal unless explicitly cleared for sharing.
- Credit work fairly. Reference your sources, attribute prior art, and acknowledge contributions from others.
- Keep the bar high on accuracy. We publish technical signal; sloppy claims damage the project's credibility and the trust of our readers.

## Not acceptable

- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Any form of harassment, public or private, targeting individuals or groups.
- Sharing another person's private information without their explicit consent.
- Misrepresenting your identity, affiliation, or the authority you act under.
- Posting under the CommitBrief brand without editorial approval.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness behavior that conflicts with this policy, contact the project owner directly:

**commitbrief@muhammetsafak.com.tr**

Reports are handled confidentially. The project owner will acknowledge the report, review the situation, and decide on the appropriate response. Actions can range from a private conversation to removal from the organization or a public channel ban, depending on severity.

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is itself a violation of this policy.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All repositories under the [CommitBrief](https://github.com/CommitBrief) GitHub organization
- Official CommitBrief channels (X, newsletter, email)
- Any space where a person is representing CommitBrief

The project owner has final authority on interpretation and enforcement within the scope above.
