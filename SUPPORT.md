# Getting Support

How you reach us depends on what you need.

## I want to read CommitBrief

Follow us on **X** at [@commitbrief](https://x.com/commitbrief). Additional channels (LinkedIn, newsletter) will be announced when they are live.

## I think a published story is wrong or misleading

Editorial corrections are taken seriously. The fastest path is email:

**commitbrief@muhammetsafak.com.tr**

Include the URL or post you are referencing, what you believe is incorrect, and a source we can verify against. We prioritize corrections that affect technical accuracy, security advice, or attribution.

## I found a bug in a CommitBrief repository

Open an issue on the relevant repository using the *Bug report* template. The form will prompt you for the information we need to reproduce the problem.

If the bug exposes a security weakness, do not file a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) instead.

## I have a feature idea

Open an issue using the *Feature request* template. Be specific about the problem you are trying to solve — proposed solutions are easier to evaluate when the underlying need is clear.

Note that CommitBrief is a closed-source product with an opinionated roadmap. We may decline feature requests that fall outside the engineering-intelligence scope, even if they are individually reasonable.

## I want to partner, advertise, or license content

Send a short note describing your organization and the kind of collaboration you have in mind to **commitbrief@muhammetsafak.com.tr**. We respond to legitimate inquiries; please do not send mass outreach.

## I am an org member and need internal help

Use the internal channels established for the organization rather than public issues. Internal coordination, design discussions, and roadmap conversations do not belong in public-facing GitHub artifacts.

## What we do not offer

- A free per-user support contract for the public CommitBrief product
- Custom intelligence work outside published channels (at this stage)
- Telephone or real-time chat support
